How to Play

Movement

Alert System

  • Clear — 0–39%
  • Caution — 40–59%
  • Alert — 60–99%, guards pursue
  • Lockdown — 100%, heist fails

Security

Avoid camera vision cones. Guards cannot be hacked — avoid them entirely. Locked doors require terminal hacks.

Controls
